DOBE Creative Agency — Bilingual Landing Page
============================================

Files:
- index.html: landing page structure and English/Arabic text
- style.css: responsive red-and-white styling plus Arabic RTL layout
- script.js: language toggle, mobile menu, and inquiry form behavior

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Use the العربية / English button in the header to switch language.

Before publishing:
- Replace hello@dobe.agency in script.js with the real business email.
- The inquiry form currently opens the visitor's email app with a prepared message; it does not save leads to a database. For real lead collection, connect a form service or backend.
- Test the page on mobile and desktop after changing the email or connecting a form.
